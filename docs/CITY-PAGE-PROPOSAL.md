Prepared 2026-11-05. Nothing here is published. No city page exists yet, and the
/service-areas/ hub links to none.

## Rules this proposal follows
- **Neighborhoods are suggestions, not coverage.** The names below come from general
  knowledge of the area. Jay decides which ones Refined Painting actually serves, and can
  replace, add or drop any of them. A neighborhood on a page is a coverage claim.
- **Neighborhoods are sections inside their city page**, never separate pages.
- **No project or photo is assigned to a city** until Jay says which Drive "House" folders
  are in which city. City name only, never street numbers.
- **No local claims** (home eras, HOA or historic-district rules, scheduling) until Jay
  supplies them.
- **Website coverage and ad targeting are separate.** All 14 cities are approved website
  cities, Seattle included. Ad targeting (Bellevue, Kirkland, Redmond, Sammamish; Seattle
  excluded) is not touched or mentioned on any page.
- "Our Services" uses the homepage's outlined photo cards. The photos illustrate the
  service. They are not labeled as projects in that city.

## What each city page needs before it goes live
1. **Approval of the neighborhood names** for that city (zero is acceptable).
2. **Projects:** which Drive "House" folders are in that city (three or more is a good
   minimum). Optional, but without it the page has no local proof.
3. **A real review** from a customer in that city, with permission to show it. Optional.
4. **Local context** Jay can speak to, one or two plain sentences. Optional.
5. **Go-ahead to publish.** A city goes live only when its entry in
   `src/content/locations/locations.ts` is set to "ready". A page with no approved
   material beyond the standard sections should not be published.
